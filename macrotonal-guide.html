Unique harmonic relationships

Three pure fourths = one cycle

Degrees 1 → 5 → 9 → cycle. Three stacked near-pure fourths close the 1500¢ cycle exactly. In standard tuning, twelve fifths are needed.

No good fifth

Nearest steps to a pure fifth (702¢) land at 625¢ and 750¢. Harmonic logic is quartal, not quintal — compare degree 6 against a standard fifth.

Above the octave

Degrees 11 and 12 exist 50¢ and 175¢ above the standard octave. The cycle closes at 1500¢ — a minor third above the octave.
